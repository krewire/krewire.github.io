# Contributing to Krewire Community Portal

This guide covers contributing to the community portal (`github.com/krewire/krewire.github.io`).

## Contribution Policies & Guides

- **[Community Contributor Guide](docs/contributing.md)** — Workflow, editing guidelines, content standards, and quality gates.
- **[Safe & Transparent AI-Assisted Contribution Policy](docs/ai-contributions.md)** — Rules for contributors using AI coding agents, transparency guidelines, secret handling, and human accountability.

## Language & Conventions

- All content, articles, and guides must be in **English**.
- The portal is built with Krewire `.kiw` DSL and compiled with `kiw build`.
- Local development: `kiw serve` or `kiw dev`.

# Contributing to Krewire Community Portal

This guide covers contributing to the community portal (`github.com/krewire/krewire.github.io`).

## Language & Conventions

- All content, articles, and guides must be in **English**.
- The portal is built with Krewire `.kiw` DSL and compiled with `kiw build`.
- Local development: `kiw serve` or `kiw dev`.
